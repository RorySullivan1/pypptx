# Stage 03 — Hardening & remaining gaps

**Goal:** make the library trustworthy on real-world decks and close the last feature gaps
that need no rendering engine — a real PowerPoint-authored round-trip corpus (v0.3.0),
read-only transitions/animations + custom layouts (v0.4.0), performance at scale and
ergonomic helpers (v0.5.0), and cross-run text find/replace (v0.6.0).

**Versions:** v0.3.0, v0.4.0, v0.5.0, v0.6.0 (see the cards). Detail each card when its
branch opens; the later cards are overviews.

**Milestone (stage exit):** the real-world corpus round-trips clean, `feature-manifest.md`
"Feature Gaps" holds only deliberate exclusions, and large-deck benchmarks meet the v0.5.0
targets.

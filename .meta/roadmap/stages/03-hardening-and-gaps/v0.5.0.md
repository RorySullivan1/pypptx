## Objective
Close the remaining MANIFEST-level feature gaps that don't require a rendering
engine: read-only access to slide transitions and animation timelines, plus
the ability to author new slide layouts on a slide master.

## Evidence (pilot run, 2026-10-09)
This card was expanded after surveying 511 decks, fetched to a scratch dir and not committed:
- Apache POI `test-data/slideshow` (95 decks, git `ae62bb5`);
- LibreOffice `sd/qa/unit/data/pptx` (403 decks, git `51ce606`);
- the committed v0.4.0 corpus (24 decks).

Microsoft PowerPoint saved 464 of the 511, per `docProps/app.xml`. What the survey showed:

- **Transitions are common, and often wrapped in `mc:AlternateContent`.**
  - 57 PowerPoint-saved decks have slide transitions. In 28 of them the transition sits inside `mc:AlternateContent`:
    - `Requires="p14"` (PowerPoint 2010) holds `p14:dur` and the PowerPoint 2010 effects;
    - `Requires="p159"` holds Morph (`p159:morph option="byObject"`, namespace `http://schemas.microsoft.com/office/powerpoint/2015/09/main`);
    - each has a `mc:Fallback` with an ECMA-376 equivalent, for example `p:fade` for Morph.
  - Effects seen: `fade` (85 plain + 18 wrapped), `zoom` 44, `wipe` 16, `wheel`, `strips`, `blinds`, `randomBar`, `dissolve`, `push`, `diamond`, `comb`, `newsflash`, `plus`, `split`, `p14:flythrough` and `p159:morph`.
  - Some decks have a `p:transition` with no child element: `spd` or `advTm` only, no effect.
  - Masters (24 decks) and layouts (31 decks) carry `p:transition` / `p:timing` too.
- **The v0.4.0 corpus is too thin for this version.** Only 3 of its 24 decks have transitions (11 of their 12 are `p:zoom`) and only 2 have entrance effects. POI's own files have far more:

  | File | Size | What it adds |
  |---|---|---|
  | `customGeo.pptx` | 1.0 MB | 48 slides with transitions; 28 click, 14 with-previous and 1 after-previous effects; 45 paragraph-level (`p:txEl`) targets |
  | `bug68703.pptx` | 60 KB | A `p14` AlternateContent transition plus an entrance effect |
  | `EmbeddedVideo.pptx` | 201 KB | An interactive (triggered) sequence and media-call effects |
  | `2411-Performance_Up.pptx` | 648 KB | An after-previous effect and paragraph builds |

  All four are POI's own sample or bug files, not website-scraped decks, so they fall under the v0.4.0 selection rule and licence decision.
- **What the animation timing trees contain** (PowerPoint-saved files):
  - Effects (`p:cTn` with `presetClass`): entrance 215, exit 30, motion path 16, emphasis 11, media call 11.
  - Node types: `clickEffect` 154, `withEffect` 107, `afterEffect` 22, `interactiveSeq` 7.
  - Behaviours: `set` 252, `anim` 173, `animEffect` 154, `animMotion` 16, `cmd` 11, `animScale` 11, `animRot` 3, `animClr` 3, `audio`/`video` 10.
  - Targets: `spTgt` 646, `txEl` 152 (by-paragraph builds), `sndTgt` 1.
  - `p:bldP` appears 126 times.
- **`presetID` is per class, and only part of it matches a Microsoft enum.**
  - Microsoft's `MsoAnimEffect` (Microsoft Learn) and LibreOffice's PPTX importer table (`oox/source/ppt/commontimenodecontext.cxx`) agree for **entrance/exit IDs 1–31** (1 Appear, 2 Fly, 3 Blinds … 10 Fade … 23 Zoom … 31 Grow and Turn). LibreOffice's wording differs for a few of them, such as 18, which Microsoft calls Strips and LibreOffice "diagonal-squares".
  - They diverge above 31. Entrance 37 is "rise-up" in LibreOffice, but `msoAnimEffectRiseUp` is 34.
  - Emphasis and motion-path IDs do not match `MsoAnimEffect` at all. Emphasis 8 is Spin, but `msoAnimEffectSpin` is 61. Path 1 is Circle, but `msoAnimEffectPathCircle` is 86.
  - ECMA-376 does not define names for these IDs.
- **Custom layouts.**
  - PowerPoint writes a user-made layout as `<p:sldLayout preserve="1" userDrawn="1">`, with no `type` attribute (so the default `cust`) and `p:cSld name`.
  - Its placeholders inherit their geometry through an empty `p:spPr`, and the footer placeholders use `idx` 10/11/12 (date/footer/slide number).
  - `preserve="1"` is on 4,134 of PowerPoint's 4,179 layouts.
  - 4,167 of the 4,180 `p:sldLayoutId`s in PowerPoint-saved decks have an `id` ≥ 2147483648; the 13 without one are all in a single deck. pypptx's `add_sldLayoutId` writes **no `id`** (used by `import_slide` today).
- **Handout masters.**
  - 70 PowerPoint-saved decks (4 in the corpus) contain a handout master.
  - No part class is registered for `CT.PML_HANDOUT_MASTER`, so pypptx loads it as an opaque blob.
- **What exists today.**
  - `CT_Slide` / `CT_SlideLayout` / `CT_SlideMaster` declare `transition` / `timing` only as untyped `ZeroOrOne`s.
  - `CT_SlideTiming`, `CT_TimeNodeList` and `CT_TLMediaNodeVideo` exist only to append the `p:video`/`p:audio` media nodes that `add_movie` / `add_audio` write.
  - `LayoutShapes` has no `add_*` methods.
  - `SlideLayouts` supports indexing, iteration, `get_by_name`, `index` and `remove`.
  - `ns.py` has `p14`/`p15` but not `p159`.
- **Verified enum values** (Microsoft Learn):
  - `PpTransitionSpeed`: Slow 1, Medium 2, Fast 3.
  - `MsoAnimTriggerType`: None 0, OnPageClick 1, WithPrevious 2, AfterPrevious 3, OnShapeClick 4, OnMediaBookmark 5.
  - `p:transition` children (Open XML SDK / [MS-PPTX] §2.3):
    - the 21 ECMA effects: blinds, checker, circle, comb, cover, cut, diamond, dissolve, fade, newsflash, plus, pull, push, random, randomBar, split, strips, wedge, wheel, wipe, zoom;
    - 19 `p14` effects: conveyor, doors, ferris, flash, flip, flythrough, gallery, glitter, honeycomb, pan, prism, reveal, ripple, shred, switch, vortex, warp, wheelReverse, window;
    - `p15:prstTrans`, `p159:morph` (seen in the corpus), `p:sndAc` and `p:extLst`.
  - Attributes: `spd`, `advClick`, `advTm` and `p14:dur`.

## Owner decisions (open, with recommendations)
1. **Corpus extension.** Commit the four POI files above, about 1.9 MB, under the existing "testing only" decision.
   - Take them from the pinned SVN revision 1938723, as the v0.4.0 files were, and add SHA-256 rows to `SOURCES.md`.
   - **Recommended: yes.** LibreOffice's decks are *not* proposed: they are MPL-2.0, which would be a new licence decision, and the POI four cover every structure seen.
2. **Animation effect names.**
   - (a) Raw IDs only (`preset_class`, `preset_id`, `preset_subtype`).
   - (b) **Recommended:** raw IDs plus `effect_type`, an `MSO_ANIMATION_EFFECT` member with Microsoft's `MsoAnimEffect` values. It is set only for entrance/exit IDs 1–31, where Microsoft's enum and LibreOffice's table agree, and is `None` otherwise.
   - (c) Name every ID from LibreOffice's table. That is third-party, and only PowerPoint itself could verify it.
3. **What a new layout contains.** **Recommended:** mirror PowerPoint's *Insert Layout*:
   - a title placeholder and date, footer and slide-number placeholders, all inheriting the master's positions;
   - `blank=True` gives a layout with no placeholders.
4. **Non-placeholder shapes on layouts** (`add_textbox`/`add_shape`/`add_picture`, e.g. a logo). **Recommended: defer to v0.5.x.**
   - `_BaseGroupShapes`' chart, movie, audio and OLE adders call `SlidePart`-only methods, so this needs its own audit.
   - Placeholders are what make a layout useful.
5. **Layout and master transitions.** **Recommended:** expose `transition` read-only on slides, layouts and masters alike, reporting exactly what each element says.
   - It does not try to resolve inheritance; whether PowerPoint applies a master's transition to slides is a human check.

## Goals
- **Transitions (read):** `Slide.transition`, `SlideLayout.transition` and `SlideMaster.transition` return a `SlideTransition`:
  - `type` (`PP_TRANSITION_TYPE`, including the `p14`/`p15` effects and `MORPH`);
  - `speed` (`PP_TRANSITION_SPEED`);
  - `duration` (`timedelta | None`, from `p14:dur`);
  - `advance_on_click` (`bool`);
  - `advance_after` (`timedelta | None`, from `advTm`);
  - `direction` (raw `str | None`);
  - `has_sound` (`bool`).

  `mc:AlternateContent` is resolved to the richer `mc:Choice` when its requirement is one of `p14`/`p15`/`p159`, and to `mc:Fallback` otherwise.
- **Animations (read):** `Slide.animations` is a sequence of `Animation`, in play order: the main sequence first, then interactive (triggered) sequences. Each `Animation` has:
  - `shape_id` and `shape` (`None` when the target is not a shape);
  - `preset_class` (`PP_ANIMATION_CLASS`), `preset_id`, `preset_subtype`, and `effect_type` per decision 2;
  - `trigger` (`MSO_ANIMATION_TRIGGER`), and `trigger_shape` for an interactive sequence;
  - `delay` and `duration` (`timedelta | None`; "indefinite" is `None`);
  - `paragraphs` (`range | None`, for a paragraph target).
- **Custom layouts:**
  - `SlideLayouts.add_slide_layout(name, *, blank=False)` mirrors `Slides.add_slide`.
  - `LayoutShapes.add_placeholder(ph_type, left=None, top=None, width=None, height=None, *, idx=None, name=None)` returns a `LayoutPlaceholder`.
  - `SlideLayouts.duplicate(layout, name=None)` mirrors `Slides.duplicate`.
  - `p:sldLayoutId@id` is allocated correctly, both for new layouts and for `import_slide`.
- **Handout master (read):**
  - Register `HandoutMasterPart` + `CT_HandoutMaster`.
  - `Presentation.handout_master` returns a `HandoutMaster | None` (shapes, placeholders, name). It is never created on read.

### 2. OXML types (`oxml/ns.py`, `oxml/simpletypes.py`, `enum/animation.py`, `oxml/transition.py`, `oxml/timing.py`)
- **Namespace:** add `p159` to `ns.py`.
- **Simple types:**
  - `ST_TransitionSpeed` (`slow`/`med`/`fast`, default `fast`);
  - `ST_TLTime` (an `unsignedInt` in ms, or `"indefinite"` → `None`);
  - `ST_TLTimeNodeType` and `ST_TLTimeNodePresetClassType`;
  - `ST_SlideLayoutId` / `ST_SlideMasterId` (2147483648 – 4294967295).
- **Enums** (new module `enum/animation.py`):
  - `PP_TRANSITION_SPEED` (`PpTransitionSpeed` values);
  - `MSO_ANIMATION_TRIGGER` (`MsoAnimTriggerType` values);
  - `PP_TRANSITION_TYPE`: one member per effect element, plus `NONE`. It has no MS API counterpart, so its values are sequential and documented as such;
  - `PP_ANIMATION_CLASS` (entr / exit / emph / path / verb / mediacall);
  - `MSO_ANIMATION_EFFECT` per decision 2.
- **`oxml/transition.py`:**
  - `CT_SlideTransition`: the attributes above, plus a choice group over the 21 + 19 + 2 effect elements, `sndAc` and `extLst`.
  - A `transition_elm()` helper that returns the effective `p:transition` of a `p:sld` / `p:sldLayout` / `p:sldMaster`: a direct child, else from `mc:AlternateContent`.
  - Reuse `oxml/shapes/shared.py`'s AlternateContent helpers where they fit.
- **`oxml/timing.py`:**
  - Read accessors for `p:par`, `p:seq`, `p:excl` and `p:cTn` (`id`, `presetClass`, `presetID`, `presetSubtype`, `nodeType`, `dur`, `grpId`), `p:stCondLst` / `p:cond` (`delay`, `evt`, `tgtEl`), `p:tgtEl` (`spTgt@spid`, `txEl/pRg`, `sldTgt`, `sndTgt`), the behaviours' `p:cBhvr`, and `p:bldLst` / `p:bldP`.
  - Move `CT_SlideTiming` / `CT_TimeNodeList` / `CT_TLMediaNode*` here from `oxml/slide.py` unchanged; `add_movie` keeps working.
- **Tests:** `cxml` unit tests for every class, and for the AlternateContent resolution:
  - a plain transition;
  - `p14` Choice;
  - `p159` Choice;
  - an unknown `Requires` falling back to `mc:Fallback`;
  - a transition with no effect child.

### 3. Transitions API (`pptx/transition.py`, `pptx/slide.py`)
- `_BaseSlide.transition` returns a `SlideTransition` over the slide element, never `None`.
- With no `p:transition`, or no effect child: `type` is `NONE`, `advance_on_click` is `True` and the rest are `None`. This leaves room for setters after 1.0.
- Reading never creates or changes XML; unit tests assert that.

### 4. Animations API (`pptx/animation.py`, `pptx/slide.py`)
- **Walk:**
  - Go from `tmRoot` to each `p:seq`: `mainSeq` first, then each `interactiveSeq` in document order.
  - Yield every `p:cTn` with a `presetClass`, in document order.
  - Trigger: `clickEffect` → ON_PAGE_CLICK, `withEffect` → WITH_PREVIOUS, `afterEffect` → AFTER_PREVIOUS, any interactive sequence → ON_SHAPE_CLICK.
  - Each interactive sequence's trigger shape comes from its `cond evt="onClick"` `spTgt`.
  - A media-call effect is listed with class `MEDIA_CALL`. The bare `p:video`/`p:audio` media nodes are not animations and are skipped.
- **Timing:**
  - `delay` is the effect node's own `stCondLst/cond@delay`, relative to its trigger, as the PowerPoint UI shows it.
  - `duration` is the longest behaviour (child `cTn` `delay` + `dur`).
  - "indefinite" or missing → `None`.
- **Target:** the first behaviour's `tgtEl`.
  - `spTgt@spid` gives `shape_id`. `shape` is looked up in `slide.shapes`, and is `None` when the shape is missing.
  - `txEl/pRg` gives `paragraphs`.
- **Tolerance:**
  - XML that LibreOffice or a hand-editor writes, without `nodeType`, still yields effects; the trigger then defaults to ON_PAGE_CLICK.
  - Malformed timing never raises from `animations`.

### 5. Handout master (`oxml/slide.py`, `parts/slide.py`, `slide.py`, `presentation.py`, `pptx/__init__.py`)
- `CT_HandoutMaster` (`p:handoutMaster`: `cSld`, `clrMap`, `hf`, `extLst`) and `HandoutMasterPart(BaseSlidePart)`, registered for `CT.PML_HANDOUT_MASTER`.
- `HandoutMaster(_BaseMaster)`.
- `Presentation.handout_master` returns `None` when there is no handout master. Unlike `notes_master`, it never creates one.
- The part changes from a blob to an `XmlPart`, so the fidelity test must stay green on the 4 corpus decks that have one.

### 6. Custom layouts (`oxml/slide.py`, `parts/presentation.py`, `parts/slide.py`, `slide.py`, `shapes/shapetree.py`)
- **Id allocation:**
  - One allocator returns max(every `sldMasterId@id` and `sldLayoutId@id` in the presentation) + 1, never below 2147483648.
  - `add_sldLayoutId(rId, id)` always writes it, which also fixes `_import_layout_and_master`.
  - Test the allocator against a deck whose ids have gaps.
- **`add_slide_layout(name, *, blank=False)`:**
  - Partname from `next_partname("/ppt/slideLayouts/slideLayout%d.xml")`.
  - `<p:sldLayout preserve="1" userDrawn="1">` with `p:cSld name`, an empty `spTree` and `p:clrMapOvr/a:masterClrMapping`.
  - Relate layout → master and master → layout; append the `sldLayoutId`.
  - Unless `blank`, add the title placeholder and the date, footer and slide-number placeholders, copying `idx`/`sz` from the master's placeholders of those types and leaving `p:spPr` empty so positions inherit.
- **`add_placeholder`:**
  - Writes a `p:sp` with `spLocks noGrp="1"` and `p:nvPr/p:ph`. The `idx` is auto-assigned as max(existing, 12) + 1, except for title / ctrTitle.
  - Gives it a `txBody` with an empty paragraph.
  - Geometry may be omitted only when the master has a placeholder of that type to inherit from; otherwise it raises `ShapeError`.
  - Name: `"<Type> Placeholder N"`, as `_next_ph_name` already builds it.
- **`duplicate(layout, name=None)`:**
  - Deep-copy the layout part with `_PartCopier(dedup=False)`; images stay shared, as `Slides.duplicate` does.
  - Relate the copy to the same master and insert it directly after the source, the way PowerPoint's *Duplicate Layout* does.
  - Default name `"1_<name>"`, PowerPoint's convention; to be confirmed by a human check.
- **Tests:**
  - Unit tests for each method.
  - A save → reload round trip: add a layout and placeholders, add a slide on it, check the slide's placeholders inherit the layout's, and check every layout/master id is unique and ≥ 2147483648.
  - Editing a duplicate leaves the source unchanged.

## Out of scope
- **Authoring** animations or transitions: read-only only; writing is deferred past v1.0.
- Resolving which transition a slide effectively plays through layout/master inheritance (decision 5).
- Effect names beyond decision 2.
- Motion-path geometry.
- Behaviour-level detail (`anim` property values, colour or rotation amounts).
- Sound or media bookmarks.
- Slideshow or preview rendering (out of project scope per `feature-manifest.md`).
- Moving layouts between masters.
- Adding or removing slide masters.
- Authoring handout or notes masters.
- Non-placeholder shapes on layouts (decision 4).
- Theme colour-scheme deep editing: v0.6.0 or later if needed.
